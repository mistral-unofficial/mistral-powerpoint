Mistral

Ask anything about your presentation.

Mistral can read and look at your slides, rewrite text, add slides, shapes, tables and images — just say what you want, e.g. “Create 5 slides about our Q3 results”, “Turn slide 3 into three cards” or “Put the attached logo top right on every slide”.

Requests go directly from PowerPoint to api.mistral.ai.

The model must support function calling
(i.e. mistral-medium-3.5, mistral-large-4).

How predictable or varied Mistral's answers are:

  • 0–0.3 for precise tasks
  • 0.4–0.6 balanced (0.4 is the default)
  • 0.7–1.0 for creative tasks
